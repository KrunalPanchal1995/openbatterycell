# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openbatterycell

**Short description:** Learn how a battery cell works: electrode potentials, series and parallel packs, the Nernst equation and discharge, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openbatterycell is a beginner-friendly learning repo about the electrochemical cell. In three short sessions you go from a lemon or Daniell cell (two metals, one voltage), to standard electrode potentials, series and parallel packs, charge and Faraday's law and a pack design with a zinc mass, to a simulation of a discharging cell with the Nernst equation, internal resistance, maximum power and a short circuit, validated against Faraday's law and the Nernst limits. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All cell parameters are illustrative and the model is educational: it does not teach how to build real lithium or lead-acid batteries, which can be dangerous. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as pulse loads, cold-weather packs and rechargeable cells.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
